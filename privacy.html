Privacy policy

Kodacam is developed by Alexei Markel. Contact alexeivmarkel@gmail.com with privacy questions.

Information processed by the app

Kodacam processes camera images and, if enabled and permitted, microphone audio on your iPhone. Recordings, imported artwork and settings are stored locally. The developer does not receive your recordings, identifiers, location or usage analytics through the app. Kodacam has no user accounts, advertising or tracking SDKs.

Permissions and your choices

Camera access is needed to record. Microphone access is optional, and you can turn sound capture off. Photos add-only access is optional and lets you save finished reels to your photo library. You can change permissions in iOS Settings.

Storage, sharing and backups

Unfinished reels remain in local app storage until exported or discarded. Exports are stored in Files and may also be saved to Photos. Apple’s device backup, iCloud and Photos settings govern any syncing of those files. When you share a reel, your chosen recipient or service receives it under that service’s policies.

Deletion

Use Discard reel to delete an unfinished cassette. Delete exports separately from Files and Photos, including Recently Deleted where applicable. Removing the app removes its local app data; exported copies, shared copies and backups may remain in their respective locations.

Support email

If you email support, your email address, message and any attachments are received through Gmail and used to respond to your request. Please avoid sending sensitive material. You may request deletion of support correspondence by emailing the same address.

This website and external links

This website does not use analytics scripts, advertising or tracking cookies. The hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP addresses and browser details, to deliver and protect the website. Opening music credits or other external links takes you to third-party websites with their own privacy policies.

Changes

Updates to this policy will appear here with a revised date. For questions or requests, contact Alexei Markel at alexeivmarkel@gmail.com.
