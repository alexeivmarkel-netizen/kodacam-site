Record, pause and resume

Tap Record to start your reel. Pause saves the current clip so you can close the app and resume later. Allow saving to finish before force-quitting. Export reel finishes the cassette. Discard reel removes an unfinished reel after confirmation.

Export and share

Choose Max quality for a ProRes MOV master, Medium quality for a detailed H.264 MP4, or Small file for a smaller H.264 MP4. For messaging apps, try Medium or Small.

Finished reels are kept in Files under On My iPhone → Kodacam. With your permission, exports also save to Photos. Use the share button to share the last export.

Camera and sound

Before recording, open Camera settings to choose the film gauge, stock, framing and Super 8 softness. Open Camera and sound to switch cameras between clips or mute sound capture. Hiss adjusts the added noise. Setting it to zero keeps the film-style sound coloration without the added hiss.

Camera unavailable or frozen

Check Camera permission in iOS Settings. If the preview stays frozen, return to the Home Screen and reopen Kodacam. If it persists, contact support with the steps that triggered it. Recording requires a compatible ProRes 422 encoder. Microphone and Photos permissions are optional.
